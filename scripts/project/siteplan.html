Website Planning Document

Site Name

LevelUp LitRPG

This name reflects the leveling systems and progression mechanics central to the LitRPG genre.

Site Purpose

This site will provide LitRPG book recommendations, reviews, and beginner guides.

Scenarios

  • What are the best LitRPG books for beginners?
  • What does leveling up mean in LitRPG stories?
  • Which series are similar to my favorite books?
